Effective Burden Context
Why the headline rate overstates the real burden
📊
Ireland applies the zero rate to most food, children's clothing, oral medicines and publications — far broader than most EU peers. Combined with the 13.5% hospitality rate and the new 9% catering rate, the effective VAT burden on lower-income households is materially below what the 23% headline suggests.
